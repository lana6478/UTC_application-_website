Manifesto / Purpose

This space is an exploration of digital fluidity. The background behind this interface is a reactive ASCII landscape—a persistent field of data that responds to your physical presence.

While the UI remains rigid and legible, the reality beneath it is in constant, unpredictable motion. Move your cursor to disturb the noise.

Use the scrollbar on the right to navigate the internal documentation of this project. The background will remain locked in place, ensuring the atmosphere is never broken.
